What changed, and why

  • Outer dashed ring + lime arc — kept pixel-for-pixel from the main mark. This is the part that says "Hakam Data Studio."
  • Radar sweep wedge replaces the play-button triangle — a scan beam, not a "press play" cue, since this feature measures and detects rather than runs a process.
  • Two concentric rings + two blips read as a literal radar screen at a glance, even at 24px in a nav bar.
  • Top lime dot — kept as-is. A small continuity detail that ties every Hakam Data Studio mark together.
