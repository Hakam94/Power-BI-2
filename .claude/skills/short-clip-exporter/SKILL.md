# Short Clip Exporter Skill (@HakamDataStudio)

Automated YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, and TikTok vertical clip extraction skill for Hakam Data Studio.

---

## 1. Features

- **Automated Hook Extraction**: Selects highest-energy 30-60 second clip segment.
- **Vertical 1080x1920 Framing**: Crops horizontal video centered on subject with dynamic framing.
- **Branded Captions & Watermark**: Burns Hakam Neon Cyan active-word captions onto the vertical video.
