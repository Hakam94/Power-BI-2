# هل الذكاء الاصطناعي سينهي وظيفة محلل البيانات؟ (standalone Arabic video)

A standalone Arabic-language video for Hakam Data Studio. **It is not part of the numbered `part-N` Power BI series** (the series' Part 7 is "Visual Calculations & Model Best Practices" on `claude/github-series-part-7-nabzpa`).

- **Status:** steps 1–2 of 5 done (idea validated, script v2 drafted). See [WORKFLOW.md](WORKFLOW.md).
- **Script:** [script_ar.md](script_ar.md) — ~14 min
- **Research:** [research/vidiq-findings.md](research/vidiq-findings.md) — VidIQ data pulled 2026-09-20

## Why this topic (evidence, not guesses)

1. **Your viewers asked for it.** On the career-story video ([NbHTRS2ke88](https://youtube.com/watch?v=NbHTRS2ke88)), viewers asked whether analyst jobs survive AI, and you replied publicly that you would make a video explaining what changed.
2. **The audience is Arabic-speaking.** Egypt, Saudi Arabia, Morocco and Algeria are ~95% of views in the 12-month country report.
3. **Format fit.** Your Arabic wins were a personal career story (37,109 views) and a beginner DAX tutorial (1,815). Arabic product-news videos flopped (33 and 175 views). So this video is a *beginner career question*, not a feature tour.
4. **Discovery is browse/suggested, not search.** For the hit video, YouTube search was ~1% of views. Title and thumbnail click-through matter more than keyword volume.

## Title

**Primary:** هل الذكاء الاصطناعي سينهي وظيفة محلل البيانات؟ وماذا يتعلم المبتدئ في 2026؟ *(VidIQ score 94)*

Scores are within 5 points, so they don't decide it. The primary title targets beginners and students, who are the people commenting. Avoid "الحقيقة الكاملة" unless the video really settles the question — the script deliberately doesn't claim that.

## Length

**12–16 minutes.** Your viewers' average view duration is ~2–2.6 minutes and your best Arabic videos ran 7–15 minutes, so extra length past ~15 minutes is unlikely to be watched.
